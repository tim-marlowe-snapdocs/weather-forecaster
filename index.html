⛅ Weather Forecaster

A tiny game about Overfitting

You run the weather desk. Each round you get 7 recorded days of temperatures — a scatter of dots. Three forecasters each fit those same dots, then project ahead to 3 coming days:

  • Straight-Line Steve — one ruler-straight line. Never chases a wobble.
  • Trend-Spotter Tess — bends to follow the real trend, but won't snake after every dot.
  • Dr. Wiggles — threads a curve through every single dot. Looks flawless on the past.

Anyone can trace dots they can already see. Your job: pick whose read on the pattern will land closest on the 3 coming days nobody has seen yet. Pick the closest forecaster for 3 points, the second-closest for 1, the furthest-off for 0.

The whole lesson: be careful of overfitting your explanation to the past. Some of what happened is noise that won't repeat, so a curve that traces every recorded dot — like Dr. Wiggles — can look flawless yet predict the future poorly. Match the forecast to the real pattern: a straight line for messy weeks, a gentle bend for a turning week, and only the busy curve when the shape is genuinely rich and tight.
